Land gets cleared for construction in Kenya, but nothing tracks how many trees "should" be replanted to offset it. So, using dummy data I created a system that can give us the estimated trees to plant after an area of land is cleared. The estimated trees to plant changes according to the different types of land cleared in a given county.
